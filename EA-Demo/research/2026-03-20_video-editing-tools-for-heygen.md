## Summary
You don't need to leave HeyGen for most edits — AI Studio handles 80–90% of polish natively. For everything else, **CapCut is the best starting point** for a beginner: free, mobile-friendly, and built for social media. OpusClip (which you've already used) can also auto-add B-roll and captions with one click.

## Key Findings

- **Start inside HeyGen** — AI Studio lets you swap B-roll, add text, transitions, and branding before export

## Deep Dive

### 1. What to Do Inside HeyGen First
Before exporting, use HeyGen's AI Studio to:
- Swap or add B-roll from HeyGen's built-in library
- Add animated text overlays, captions, and transitions
- Apply logo/branding and background music
- Set the right dimensions (9:16 portrait for social, 16:9 for YouTube/LinkedIn)

This handles the majority of polish without needing another tool.

### 2. Best Beginner Editing Tools (After Export)

| Tool | Best For | Price | Ease of Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CapCut** | Social media edits, captions, effects | Free / $8/month Pro | 9.5/10 — easiest |
| **OpusClip** | Auto B-roll, clips, captions, scheduling | Free tier / paid | 9/10 — you already know it |
| **Wondershare Filmora** | More control, templates, guided edits | $50/year | 9/10 |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Professional grade, free, audio tools | Free | 7/10 — steeper curve |
| **Adobe Premiere Elements** | Guided edits, 3 skill levels | ~$100 one-time | 9/10 |

**Recommendation for George:** Start with **CapCut** for quick social edits. Use **OpusClip** when you want auto-everything (B-roll, captions, repurposing). Upgrade to Filmora when you want more creative control.

### 4. What OpusClip Can Do (Beyond Clipping)
Since you've used it before, you may not know it also does:
- **Auto B-roll insertion** — adds relevant stock footage automatically (Pro feature)
- **Animated captions** — 97%+ accuracy, customizable style
- **Brand kit** — applies your colors, fonts, and logo
- **Virality scoring** — ranks which clips will perform best
- **Direct publishing** — posts to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- **Auto-reframing** — keeps your face centered in vertical format

### 5. Recommended Post-Production Workflow

```
HeyGen (generate + basic polish)
    ↓
Export MP4
    ↓
CapCut or OpusClip (B-roll, captions, effects, music)
    ↓
Export in correct format per platform:
  - 9:16 → Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, RedNote
  - 16:9 → LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube
    ↓
Publish
```
