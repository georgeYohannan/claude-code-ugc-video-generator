# HeyGen — Video to Social Media Workflow

**Date:** 2026-03-20
**Query:** How to use an iPhone-recorded video + script in HeyGen to generate and distribute social media content across Facebook, Instagram, RedNote, LinkedIn, and YouTube.

---

## Summary
Upload your iPhone video to HeyGen to create a custom AI avatar, sync it with your script, then export in the right format for each platform. The whole process takes minutes once your avatar is set up.

---

## Step-by-Step in HeyGen

1. **Create your custom avatar**
   - Go to HeyGen dashboard → **Create Your Avatar** → Upload your iPhone video (trim to ~2 min)
   - HeyGen generates a video avatar that mimics your likeness and movements

2. **Input your script**
   - Paste your script into AI Studio's text editor
   - Use the AI Script Generator to tighten it if needed (aim for 15–60 sec for social)
   - Add pauses, pronunciation notes, and scene breaks as needed

3. **Customize for each platform**
   - Set video dimensions: **9:16 portrait** for Instagram/RedNote/YouTube Shorts · **16:9 landscape** for LinkedIn/Facebook/YouTube
   - Add backgrounds, text overlays, brand colors, music, and auto-captions
   - On mobile: use **Edit Styles** (one-tap templates) for quick social-ready formatting

4. **Generate & export**
   - Preview → Generate → Download MP4 or grab shareable link
   - Repeat export for each format (portrait + landscape)

---

## Platform Cheat Sheet

| Platform | Format | Key Tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | 16:9 or square | Post natively, add caption summary, professional tone |
| **Instagram** | 9:16 (Reels) | Add music/stickers in app, post at peak times, use hashtags |
| **RedNote** | 9:16 | Hook in first 3 seconds, trending sounds, vertical only |
| **LinkedIn** | 16:9 | Professional tone, add text summary, tag relevant connections |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 9:16 | Upload natively, optimize title + description |
| **YouTube (long-form)** | 16:9 | Add thumbnail, end screen, chapters if longer |
