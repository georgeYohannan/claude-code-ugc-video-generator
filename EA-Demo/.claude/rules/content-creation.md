# Content Creation Rules

## Social Media
- Platform voices: LinkedIn (professional + insight-driven) · X/Twitter (punchy, direct) · Instagram (visual-first, caption supports the image)
- Always include relevant hashtags
- Repurpose content across platforms — don't rewrite from scratch
- Every post should tie back to George's authority as an AI consultant or generate interest in GYanTech AI

## Blog Posts
- Structure: Hook intro → Key points → Actionable takeaway
- Length: 800–1,500 words unless specified
- SEO-friendly headings (H2/H3)
- Output in Markdown

## YouTube Scripts
- Structure: Hook (first 30 sec) → Main content → CTA
- Include B-roll notes and on-screen text suggestions in brackets
- Written to be spoken, not read — conversational delivery

## Content Workflow Order
1. YouTube script
2. Blog post based on the video
3. LinkedIn article summary
4. Social posts (X + Instagram)
5. Email newsletter snippet
